Mechanical Engineering Graduate · CFD & Multiphase Flow Researcher

Investigating complex fluid phenomena through computational modeling, numerical simulation, and data-driven approaches.

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with research experience in computational fluid dynamics, multiphase flow, and thermal-fluid sciences. My work combines VOF-based numerical simulations with data-driven modeling to investigate complex fluid phenomena, particularly droplet impact dynamics. I have also explored Augmented Neural ODEs as surrogate models for predicting fluid behavior and am interested in extending computational approaches to heat transfer, evaporation, and interdisciplinary engineering problems.
